# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, assume good faith, acknowledge uncertainty, and prioritize reproducible evidence over performance claims. Do not harass, insult, threaten, disclose private information, or use sexualized language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method published on the repository profile. Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect reporter privacy as far as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
